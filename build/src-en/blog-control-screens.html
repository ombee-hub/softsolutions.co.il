Control and monitoring screens

Softsolutions·February 28, 2022·1 min read

Almost every business process eventually needs a clear, sharp way to present data to users and managers. Dedicated screens that show the essence of your business information are part of continuously improving workflows and controls.

A large screen in the service department showing open calls, a chart of warehouse tasks in progress for the warehouse manager, and much more – this is exactly what the Portal Builder and its rotating display mode were created for.

Rotating display screens are designed to present information clearly: records refresh automatically and rotate on screen at a set pace ("departure board" mode). It's a highly effective way to show data on display screens in the warehouse, at the service center or on managers' workstations.

Setting up a rotating display screen

  • Define the screen's columns, query conditions and so on – just like any regular screen.
  • In the dedicated sub-screen "Rotating display screen settings", define 2 parameters:
    • Records per page – how many records are shown on each page.
    • Page interval (seconds) – how long records are shown before moving to the next page.
  • Data is reloaded at the start of each page cycle. For example, with 20 service calls displayed 10 per page, a new query runs only after all 20 calls have been shown.

Good to know

  • On a rotating display screen, you can't click a record to open its detailed view.
  • The total number of records is shown at the top of the page.
  • We recommend displaying the browser in full-screen mode (F11).
  • You can set up several tabs with different data and rotate between them automatically using standard browser extensions.
