The builder is here

Softsolutions·November 11, 2020·1 min read

Turn any Priority screen into an app or a web page – no development and no interfaces – for PCs, smartphones and tablets.

  • Native apps for field teams.
  • Landing pages to embed in your company website or send by SMS.
  • Portals for a wide range of uses.
  • A customer website for service calls, reports, orders and more.

No development, remarkably easy

  • Full support for custom screens and in-house developments.
  • No interfaces and no development.
  • Simply choose a screen and the columns to display – and get to work.

It's a Native system, so it can use every device capability: GPS, customer signatures, the camera, navigation software, voice dictation in the field and more.
