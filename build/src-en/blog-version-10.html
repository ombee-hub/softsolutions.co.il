So what's new in this version?

Softsolutions·April 7, 2021·1 min read

Welcome to the new version 10.0.0 of the App Builder.

We're happy to share the many new features in this version, developed together with all of our customers. The update will roll out automatically to the app stores in the coming days, and from there to your field teams.

A new look

  • A new, clean and consistent look across all of your organization's apps.
  • A new card and record view that puts the focus where it belongs for efficient work.
  • Quick buttons for instant access to sub-screens and common actions.
  • Full support for links between screens (LINK) – work seamlessly without going back to menus.
  • Better control over field placement in the view.
  • Work environment display, including support for colors defined in Priority.

There's no place like home

  • A new, intuitive home screen.
  • Fully dynamic display of system data – based on the customer, work environment and contact.
  • Quick-launch buttons that go straight to a work screen or a specific record.
  • Support for customer and user messages, including read receipts, logs and more.

Fast, efficient and secure

We added. A lot. Improved login mechanisms, 2FA security support, connection-loss alerts and better stability. Everything is now faster, with a consistent interface across all screens and a range of new features – all to make field work more efficient.

  • View attachments and documents inside the app, with no additional software.
  • Improved support for customer questionnaires, including quick navigation and several display layouts.
  • A completely new actions menu that brings everything important into one place.
  • Quick actions, plus/minus buttons for quantity fields and auto-save.
  • Free-text formatting support, including previous texts and editing existing text.
  • Improved image display.
  • Automatic focus on the last record.

Best regards,
Softsolutions
