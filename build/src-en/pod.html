The field delivery app

End-to-end supply chain management – from the warehouse to the customer's signature

The Priority field delivery app gives you an advanced system for managing your distribution operations. It provides complete, controlled management of the delivery process – loading and unloading – including navigation with maps or Waze.

On arrival, drivers can have delivery notes signed electronically and documented in the system immediately. The app supports continuous, accurate work even when goods are not delivered, manages driver tasks such as cash collection, and handles planned or ad hoc returns.
