Privacy policy

Your privacy matters to us. This page explains what information we collect, how we use it and what your rights are.

Last updated: October 2026

1. General

This website is operated by Softsolutions Ltd. ("the Company", "we"), 2 HaMada St., Yokneam, Israel. This policy applies to information collected on www.softsolutions.co.il and its English version. It does not apply to information collected offline or through other channels.

By using the website, you agree to the terms of this policy.

2. Information we collect

  • Information you provide – when you use the contact form: full name, company name, phone number, email address, area of interest and the content of your message, and whether you asked to receive email updates. Contacting us directly by phone or email may also include such information.
  • Technical information – like any website, our hosting server records log files with data such as IP address, browser and device type, internet service provider, date and time of the visit and the pages viewed. This information does not identify you personally and is used to operate and secure the website and to analyze general trends.
  • Accessibility settings – the settings you choose in the accessibility menu are stored only in your browser on your device (localStorage) and are never sent to us.

You are not legally required to provide any information. However, without contact details we will not be able to respond to your inquiry.

3. How we use information

  • To respond to inquiries, schedule demos and provide quotes.
  • To provide service and support to our customers.
  • To send updates and marketing content by email – only with your consent. You can withdraw your consent at any time using the unsubscribe link in every message or by contacting us.
  • To operate, improve and secure the website and prevent misuse.
  • To comply with legal requirements.

4. Sharing information with third parties

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share information with third parties only:

  • With service providers acting on our behalf (for example website hosting and email services), and only as needed to provide their service.
  • With authorized Priority business partners – only when needed to handle your inquiry, and with your consent.
  • When required by law, court order or a competent authority.
  • In the event of a merger, acquisition or transfer of the Company's business – provided the receiving party commits to this policy.

5. Third-party services on the website

The website includes external services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Google Fonts – the website's fonts are loaded from Google's servers, which may receive your IP address.
  • Google Maps – a map embedded on the contact page, which may set Google cookies.
  • Links to social networks (LinkedIn, Facebook, YouTube), to the demo app downloads, to the support center and to the FileSmile website. Following these links takes you to sites we do not control.

6. Cookies

The website itself does not use advertising or tracking cookies. External services embedded in it (such as the map) may use their own cookies. On your first visit a cookie banner is shown: accepting allows these services to load, and declining blocks them – for example, the map on the contact page will only load if you click to load it. Your choice is saved in your browser on your device. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; some services may not work properly if you do.

7. Security and retention

We take reasonable, industry-standard measures to protect information from unauthorized access, use or disclosure. However, no security measure is completely foolproof. Information is kept for as long as it is needed for the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by law.

8. Your rights

Under the Israeli Privacy Protection Law, 5741‑1981, you may:

  • Review the information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct information that is inaccurate, incomplete, unclear or out of date, or to delete it.
  • Ask to be removed from our mailing list.

To make such a request, contact us using the details in section 11. We will respond within the time required by law.

9. Minors

The website is intended for organizations and professionals and is not directed at minors. We do not knowingly collect information about minors.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The binding version is the one published on the website, and the date of the last update appears at the top of this page.
