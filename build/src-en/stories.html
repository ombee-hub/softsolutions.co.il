A new weighing and production reporting system

A new weighing and production reporting system from Softsolutions is now live across the sites of MasterFood – one of Israel's leading importers, marketers and distributors of meat and fish.

New Cinema

App and customer website builder

Softsolutions completed the rollout of its app and customer website builder at New Cinema. The project delivered several apps for the company's employees, plus a mechanism that texts the company's customers so they can place orders from their phones – no app installation required.

Our field agents give their customers better service, including the ability to track shipments on their way to the store and to end customers. All in all, there isn't a single area of our business that the app builder hasn't moved forward.Nadav Kirshner, IT Manager
Atlantium

Atlantium deployed a builder-based portal that keeps its users around the world updated in real time, straight from Priority.

We connected users spread across the globe quickly and easily. The basic implementation took less than a week, and since then the portal has been simple and convenient to work with.Roi Shemer, VP Operations
