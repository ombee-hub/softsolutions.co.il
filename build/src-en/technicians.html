Service call management

The process you defined – enforced precisely in the field

The system gives your service team full control of every call, whether it was opened by the call center or directly in the field. The app enforces the service call workflow exactly as defined by service management.

All call data is available in the field, along with a complete call history for the customer and even for the specific device. The system also includes advanced options for service teams, such as customer questionnaires, checklists, purchase requisitions and more.

The system includes

Every tool your service team needs, in the van and on site

  • Open service calls from the field
  • Full management of the call workflow, based on the statuses defined in Priority
  • Technician inventory management, including serialized devices
  • Report spare parts per call with instant inventory updates
  • Working hours reports
  • Fault reports, damage photos and customer questionnaires
  • Customer signature on every call, including location at the time of signing
  • Inventory transfers between service vehicles and goods receipts directly to the vehicle
