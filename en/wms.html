Priority's WMS module

Every inventory movement – managed and controlled

Priority's warehouse management system integrates fully and easily with Priority ERP and requires no additional interfaces.

Built-in features include picking, packing and shipping, receiving and putaway, replenishment task waves, tracking and traceability, and support for mobile devices, barcodes, RFID tags and more.

An app for the warehouse team

A powerful app for a fast, accurate warehouse

Automatic inventory receiving, scanning and access to the relevant information for every action are critical to fast, accurate warehouse operations.

The WMS app gives you full flexibility in designing warehouse workflows, data controls and work allocation – with no development required and immediate rollout on the floor. Simply select which data to display for each task type.

Capabilities

What you get in the warehouse

  • Split-screen view: picking line details alongside instant inventory scanning
  • Instant barcode recognition – by part number, lot, serial number or international standards
  • Product images for quick identification
  • Support for mandatory scanning, selection from a list and more
  • Instant access to any sub-level form, including custom screens
  • Run user reports independently of the current task
  • Business rules and custom code work instantly, with no adjustments
  • Task planning data displayed according to your organization's needs
