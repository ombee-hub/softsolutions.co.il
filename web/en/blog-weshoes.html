The App Builder at WeShoes

Softsolutions·November 15, 2020·1 min read

Softsolutions Ltd. recently completed the rollout of its app and customer website builder at New Cinema.

The project delivered several apps for the company's employees, plus a mechanism that texts the company's customers so they can place orders from their phones – no app installation required.

Before the system went live, our offices were flooded with phone calls from field staff asking for information. Today everyone generates the report or views the data they need on their own.

Beyond saving time, our field agents give their customers better service, including tracking packages on their way to the store and to end customers. The calls about simple things that wasted valuable time have almost completely disappeared.

We also used the system to collect information from employees of companies we have product contracts with – through a landing page sent to those employees directly by SMS.

There isn't a single department that the App Builder hasn't moved forward – from giving customers access to their own data to entering orders directly into our Priority system.

Nadav Kirshner, IT Manager, New Cinema

On one hand, the project let New Cinema's customers define product baskets for each of their employees directly in New Cinema's Priority system. On the other, it gave instant SMS access through which every employee of those customers can order their products – while fully preserving system controls and complying with customer contracts.

The entire implementation went smoothly and quickly, using all the business logic already in New Cinema's Priority system, with no custom development needed.

Elad Lagun, CEO, Softsolutions
